Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 9, 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how Calculator X ("we," "our," or "the App") handles your information. Your privacy is our priority.

1. No Personal Data Collection

Calculator X is designed to function primarily as a local utility. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). Your calculations, history, and voice pack settings remain strictly on your device.

2. Data Used Locally

The App uses the following data locally on your device:

  • Calculation History: Stored in the App's local database and can be deleted by you at any time.
  • Voice Packs: Audio files you record or select are stored in your device's internal storage and are not uploaded to any servers.

3. Internet Usage & Third-Party Services

The App requires Internet access for the following specific purposes:

  • Currency Conversion: To fetch the latest exchange rates from public APIs. This data is used only for the calculation and is not linked to your identity.
  • freeRASP Security: We use freeRASP to protect the App from tampering and malware. It may check for device security risks (like rooting), but it does not collect personal user data.

4. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Storage: To save your calculation history as a .txt file upon your request.
  • Internet: To update currency rates.

5. Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any data, our App is safe for use by children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
